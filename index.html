Problem Statement

Tomato is a highly perishable agricultural product. During transport and storage, quality can decline rapidly due to environmental and mechanical factors. This causes economic losses and reduces product marketability.

Postharvest loss risk: product quality drops quickly after transport and during storage.
Operational challenge: sellers need fast, accurate decisions about whether tomatoes should be sold, monitored, or rejected.
Research need: AI can transform postharvest data into a practical decision-support system.

Why This Study Matters

For logistics
Helps evaluate transport-related quality risk.
For storage
Supports storage management and shelf-life decisions.
For marketability
Predicts whether the product is marketable, needs fast sale, or is unmarketable.

❌

Scientific Basis

Tomato quality is influenced by several postharvest factors. Longer distance, higher temperature, longer storage duration, and vibration all contribute to deterioration.

  • Longer transport distance increases damage risk.
  • Higher storage temperature accelerates deterioration.
  • Longer storage duration reduces overall quality.
  • Transport vibration contributes to postharvest loss.

Key Findings

  • The regression model predicted quality losses close to the real values.
  • The classification model assigned tomatoes into practical marketability classes.
  • Storage day had the strongest impact in the current prototype dataset.
  • Severe conditions such as 205 km, 22°C, 12 days, and vibration 1.3 produced an Unmarketable result.
