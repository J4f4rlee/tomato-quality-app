Problem Statement

Why tomato quality prediction matters

Tomato is a highly perishable agricultural product. During transport and storage, quality can decline rapidly due to environmental and mechanical factors, causing economic loss and reducing marketability.

  • Postharvest loss risk increases after transport and during storage.
  • Sellers need fast and accurate quality decisions.
  • AI can transform postharvest data into a practical decision-support system.

Project Objectives

🤖

Develop an AI-based system

Build a machine learning model that predicts tomato market status automatically from transport and storage data.

🍅

Evaluate tomato quality

Assess the effect of transport distance, temperature, storage duration, and vibration on postharvest quality.

📊

Support decisions

Provide a practical tool for logistics, warehouse management, and sales planning.

Input Parameters

📦

Transport Distance

Distance travelled in kilometers.

🌡️

Storage Temperature

Storage condition in degrees Celsius.

📅

Storage Day

Number of days after transport.

📳

Vibration Level

Relative vibration intensity during transport.

Output Classes

✅

Marketable

Suitable for normal sale conditions and still retains acceptable quality.

⚠️

Fast Sale Required

Still saleable, but quality is declining and should be sold quickly.

❌

Unmarketable

Predicted to have poor quality and no longer suitable for market sale.

Scientific Basis

  • Longer transport distance increases damage risk.
  • Higher storage temperature accelerates deterioration.
  • Longer storage duration reduces overall quality.
  • Transport vibration contributes to postharvest loss.

Key Findings

  • The regression model predicted quality losses close to the real values.
  • The classification model assigned tomatoes into practical marketability classes.
  • Storage day had the strongest impact in the current prototype dataset.
  • 205 km, 22°C, 12 days, and vibration 1.3 produced an Unmarketable result.
